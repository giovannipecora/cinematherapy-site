What science says

Art is good for you.Really.

Making art and enjoying beauty has effects you can measure, on body and mind.

i.

Less stress, less pain

Art and beauty help lower cortisol, the stress hormone, and ease chronic pain or the pain of a hospital stay.

ii.

Feelings that find words

Painting, music, theatre, writing and dance give a voice to emotions and wounds that are hard to put into words.

iii.

A sharper mind

Especially in older people and those with mild neurocognitive disorders, art helps memory, attention and spatial skills.

iv.

Your body says thanks

Some studies, also cited by Johns Hopkins University, point to positive effects on the immune system and the heart.

Sources · IBSA Foundation · Istituto Beck · JCMH · Casa di Cura San Rossore
